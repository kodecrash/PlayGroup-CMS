Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about admissions, enrollment deadlines, supplies, or safety?

We welcome children from 1.5 years (Toddlers) up to 6 years old (Kindergarten Prep). We also provide flexible after-school enrichment and weekend clubs for graduated early primary students.

We maintain strict low-ratio environments: 1:4 for Toddler Explorers, 1:6 for Pre-Kindergarten, and 1:8 for Kindergarten Scholars, ensuring every child receives attentive, loving guidance.

Yes! We provide fresh, chef-prepared hot lunches and wholesome morning and afternoon snacks made with organic ingredients. We strictly accommodate all food allergies, dairy-free, and vegetarian needs.

We use a secure, private Parent Portal App where you receive real-time check-in notifications, daily activity photo albums, nap times, meal logs, and direct chat with your child's teachers.

Our campus features biometric security entry gates, 24/7 HD CCTV cameras covering indoor and outdoor zones, full background checks for all personnel, and pediatric first-aid certification for every staff member.
